How to use the Camera tool

Shoot directly in the browser with 1×–10× zoom and land the shot straight in the editor.

Jan 7, 2026 3 min read By the editors

What this tool does

The Camera tool opens a live view of your device's rear camera in a full-screen modal. Zoom controls (1×, 2×, 5×, 10× pills plus a slider) let you get closer to the subject without moving. The shutter captures a still frame and drops it straight into the editor — ready for Enhance, Crop, or any other tool.

When to use it

  • Quickly digitising a document or receipt without opening another app.
  • Photographing an object for reverse image search (Google Lens hand-off).
  • Shooting a photo that you want to immediately crop or enhance.
  • Any situation where you don't already have a photo on disk.

Step-by-step

  1. Click Open Camera on the homepage (or in the Camera tool panel).
  2. Grant the browser permission to use your camera when prompted.
  3. Point at your subject. Use the 1×, 2×, 5×, 10× pills or the slider to zoom.
  4. Click the round shutter button at the bottom.
  5. The shot lands in the editor. Continue with any other tool.

Tips for best results

  • Even lighting. Stand near a window or under soft overhead light. A single harsh light source creates strong shadows.
  • Hold the phone parallel to the subject. Especially for documents — a tilted phone produces trapezoid distortion that cropping can't fix.
  • Zoom in when you can. 2× zoom for documents gets you closer to the text without moving physically closer — which reduces shadow.
  • Tap the camera view to focus (if your browser supports it — Chrome and Safari do).

Common mistakes

  • Shadow across the subject. Your body or the phone casts a shadow. Move to indirect light.
  • Tilted phone. The perspective skews. Hold the device flat and parallel to the subject.
  • Forgetting to grant camera permission. If nothing happens, check that the site has camera access in your browser's site settings.
  • Using the wrong camera. The tool requests the rear camera by default, but on some laptops only the front camera is available — the preview will be mirrored, which is fine for photos of you but wrong for documents.
