How to rotate and flip a photo

Fix sideways phone shots, mirrored selfies, and upside-down scans in one click.

Jan 13, 2026 3 min read By the editors

What this tool does

The Rotate & Flip tool gives you four one-click orientation fixes: Rotate Left 90°, Rotate Right 90°, Flip Horizontal, and Flip Vertical. Unlike most tools in this app, these actions commit immediately — no Apply step. Whatever you click is baked into the current image instantly.

When to use it

  • Phone photos taken sideways (very common with landscape shots opened on desktop).
  • Selfies or mirrored photos where text appears backwards.
  • Scanned pages that came out upside-down.
  • Photos with a horizon that's slightly tilted (rotate 90° twice if you need to undo a wrong rotation).

Step-by-step

  1. Upload your image and open the Rotate & Flip tool.
  2. Click one of the four buttons. The change applies immediately to the image.
  3. Click again if you need more rotation. Two clicks of Right 90° equals 180°.
  4. Download when done, or continue with other tools.

Tips for best results

  • Rotate before you crop. Cropping to a ratio and then rotating produces the wrong proportions. Fix orientation first.
  • Flip is not rotate. If the image is upside-down, rotate. If text appears backwards, flip. Mixing them up creates the "mirror-world" look.
  • Every click is permanent. If you overshoot, click the opposite direction the same number of times to undo.

Common mistakes

  • Rotating by 180° twice. You're back where you started. Use a single 180° = two clicks of 90° with awareness of which direction you're going.
  • Flipping when you meant to rotate. Flip mirrors the image; rotate changes its orientation. Different results entirely.
  • Rotating after adding text. The text rotates with the image and becomes sideways. Rotate first, then add text.
