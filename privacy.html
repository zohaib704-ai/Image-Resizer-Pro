Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Your images

Every image you process on ImageResizer Pro is handled entirely in your browser. Images are never uploaded, stored, transmitted, or viewed by us. When you close the tab, all working data is discarded.

2. Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertising to keep the tools free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Third-party libraries and services

  • Cropper.js (CDN) — for the crop tool.
  • Tesseract.js (CDN) — for OCR. Language models are downloaded once per language.
  • Formspree — only if you submit the contact form.
  • Google Fonts — for Fraunces and Inter fonts.
  • Google Lens — only if you click the Lens tool and open the link.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. They may log your IP address as part of normal web traffic.

4. Contact form

If you submit the contact form, the information you enter is sent to us via Formspree. This is used only to reply to you and is never sold or shared for marketing.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to count visitors in aggregate. No personal information is collected.

6. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
