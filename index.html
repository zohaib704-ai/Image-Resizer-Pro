02 / FAQ

Answers, briefly.

Do my images get uploaded?

No. Every operation happens in your browser via JavaScript. Your images never touch a server, and we never see them. Close the tab and everything is gone.

What happens if my camera doesn't work?

Some browsers require HTTPS. On Vercel you're already on HTTPS, so it should work after you grant permission. On iOS Safari, only one camera stream can be active at a time — close other camera tabs first.

Is this really free?

Yes. The site is supported by non-intrusive advertising, so every tool stays free. No accounts, no paywalls.

Which formats can I upload and export?

Upload JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, or BMP. Export as JPEG, PNG, or WEBP.
